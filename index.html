What is SubReceipt?

SubReceipt (inspired by Receiptify) turns your recurring monthly and annual subscriptions—like Netflix, Spotify, ChatGPT Plus, iCloud, and Xbox Game Pass—into a shareable thermal store receipt. Everything runs 100% locally in your browser with zero bank login required.

How is the 10-Year S&P 500 Value Calculated?

Subscription creep silently drains wealth. We calculate the future compound value of your monthly subscription total using the historical inflation-adjusted S&P 500 average return of 8% per year compounded monthly over 120 months (10 years).

How to Reduce Subscription Fatigue

Use our built-in Kill-Switch Simulator in Step 2: uncheck services you barely use to immediately see how much cash you save per year. Rotating streaming services one month at a time can cut your entertainment bill by up to 65%.
